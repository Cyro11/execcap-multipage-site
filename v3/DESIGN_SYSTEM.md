# ExecCap / Refined direction

Simple, elegant, and polished. Navy, blue, white, and pale backgrounds retain the ExecCap identity. DM Serif Display headlines and Roboto body text use clear hierarchy and comfortable line lengths. Layouts rely on spacing, fine rules, editorial service/article lists, and a coordinated set of architectural photographs.

The footer uses a pale blue background with a subtle woven CSS texture. Contact sits beside two grouped navigation bands; the official LinkedIn icon anchors the social link. All diagonal link arrows are removed. Four firm capabilities form quadrants around a small decorative diamond, with equal-height rows and a readable vertical layout below 620px. Image-led Insights cards use one featured note and two supporting notes. Article headers pair the title with the corresponding image. Internship signup pairs the form with a linked learning feature, so the supporting column has a useful purpose throughout its height. Stock photography is illustrative and does not represent an ExecCap office.

Motion is limited to a gentle 700ms opacity reveal with an 8px upward movement as sections enter the viewport. It runs once and respects reduced motion. Navigation remains native. Content stays visible without JavaScript or IntersectionObserver support, and keyboard focus reveals the focused section immediately.

Reference websites reviewed: https://www.evercore.com/ and https://www.generalatlantic.com/. Their restrained financial-services presentation informed the direction; no layouts, photography, metrics, or copy were copied.

Capabilities and process lead the firm presentation. Accurate leadership details remain available in a supporting disclosure. No headcount, offices, clients, or results are invented.
