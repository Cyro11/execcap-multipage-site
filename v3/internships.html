Program questions

A few things
to know.

Is the program currently accepting applications?+

This edition does not announce an application window. Contact the team for current availability; the signup above is a preview.

Is registering interest an application?+

Interest registration and a formal application are separate steps. Registering interest would only request updates, without a promise of admission or a role.

What does the program cover?+

The training resources cover acquisition fundamentals, industry research, financial analysis, sourcing, deal structure, diligence, and transition planning.

Are internship and training roles the same?+

The program has different participation tracks. Responsibilities, eligibility, and terms are confirmed with the team before participation.
