How it works

A clear process.
Room for judgment.

An acquisition is a sequence of decisions. The process keeps the business, the operator, and the capital connected as those decisions become more specific.

01Thesis→02Sourcing→03Terms→04Capital→05Diligence→06Transition→

The acquisition journey

01Define the thesis02Source and screen03Shape the deal04Align capital05Test the business06Close and transition
01

Define the thesis

What kind of business fits you?

Identify the industries, business characteristics, and operating strengths that should guide the search.

Working toward

A focused acquisition thesis and target criteria.

02

Source and screen

Which opportunities deserve your time?

Organize the search, evaluate fit, and identify the questions that need to be answered before progressing.

Working toward

A prioritized pipeline and consistent screening approach.

03

Shape the deal

Can the terms support the plan?

Work through the price, proposed structure, seller transition, and responsibilities alongside the business case.

Working toward

A clear view of key terms and open questions.

04

Align capital

Who is backing the opportunity?

Discuss financing options, investor alignment, governance, and economics in the context of the acquisition.

Working toward

A capital plan to evaluate with the relevant partners.

05

Test the business

Does the evidence support the thesis?

Coordinate financial, legal, and operational diligence with the relevant specialists and keep findings attached to the acquisition decision.

Working toward

A documented view of risks, findings, and the decision to proceed.

06

Close and transition

What will ownership look like on day one?

Prepare for the handoff, communication with employees and customers, and the first operating priorities.

Working toward

A practical transition and first-100-days plan.

Progress includes knowing
when to walk away.

Timing varies with the search and the transaction. The right next step can be further investigation, a different structure, or a decision to stop.
